What am I seeing? The model takes your current sensor readings (wind speed, temperature, frequency, power) and estimates how much power this turbine will produce at 5 future time steps \u2014 from 10 minutes to 24 hours ahead. Each prediction comes with a confidence interval (the range shown below the value) representing the 95% likely range. The further ahead we predict, the wider the uncertainty.
